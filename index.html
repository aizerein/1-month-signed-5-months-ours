A letter from Draz

My Love,

I don't know how to start this, so I'll just say it. You're here. Every time I think about that, it still stops me.

Five months. One of them as your husband. And I still catch myself saying the word softly, like it might disappear if I say it too loud.

Before you, I was a closed door. I thought that was strength.

You didn't knock. You just stood there. And somehow the door remembered what it was for.

You changed me.

Not loudly. The way a single flower changes a dark room. It doesn't ask permission. It just makes the dark feel like it was waiting.

I used to think being understood was a story people told themselves to feel less alone. Then you looked at me, and I didn't have to explain anything. Not one thing.

You are my calm. You are my storm. I need both, and only you carry both.

Roses have thorns. People forget the thorns are part of it. I never wanted you softer. I never wanted you smaller. I wanted you exactly as you bled into my life.

I don't believe in accidents anymore. Something older than me knew your name long before I did.

Time is different now. It used to just pass. Now it stays. It's warm, and I'm afraid to waste a single second of it.

You chose me. Out of everyone, out of every other way your life could have gone. You chose me.

And I chose you back. I'd choose you in every version of every world.

Mine. Yes. I said it. I'm not sorry.

You're the one thing I will never let go of.

...I'm a little scared of how much I need you. I wasn't going to write that down.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for being my wife.

I love you. I'm not done saying it.

— Draz
